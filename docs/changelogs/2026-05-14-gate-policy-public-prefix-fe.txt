E. SUPER ADMIN BYPASS — ĐÃ XÓA
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Trước: Gate::before cho Super Admin → bypass mọi Policy.
Giờ:   Super Admin không bypass. Phải có role chair/operator/participant
       trên meeting cụ thể mới thao tác được các action gắn meeting.

→ Catalog/CRUD admin level (list meetings, tạo meeting, dashboard) — Super Admin
   vẫn được vì dùng Spatie permission.
→ In-meeting control (Tab 6-8 + cast vote, complete discussion, ...) — admin
   phải tự gán mình làm chair/operator trước khi can thiệp.

F. POLICY CLASSES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
File: app/Modules/Meeting/Policies/

  MeetingPolicy
    viewPublic        — ?User, Meeting  (guest/auth): meeting public + published
    viewParticipant   — User, Meeting   : user có role chair/op/participant
    participate       — User, Meeting   : user có role (alias cho self-action)
    operate           — User, Meeting   : chair OR operator
    manageAttendance  — User, Meeting   : chair OR operator
    endEarly          — User, Meeting   : chair OR operator
    highlight         — User, Meeting   : chair OR operator

  MeetingDiscussionRegistrationPolicy
    view / update / delete  — owner đại biểu OR chair/op
    complete                — chair OR operator

  MeetingVoteTopicPolicy
    view                    — user có role trong meeting cha
    open / close            — chair OR operator
    cast                    — participant OR chair (NOT operator — per spec)

  MeetingVoteResponsePolicy
    view                    — owner OR chair/op
    delete                  — chair OR operator

  MeetingAttendancePolicy
    view                    — owner OR chair/op
    approve / reject        — chair OR operator

  MeetingParticipantPolicy
    respond                 — chính đại biểu (attendee.user_id === user.id)
    view                    — chair/op HOẶC chính đại biểu

Quy ước check: KHÓA NGOẠI của meeting (chairperson_meeting_attendee_id,
operator_meeting_attendee_id) + LIST participants(participant.attendee.user_id).
